Why Kitsiso Exists

Kitsiso combines local farming realities, accessible design, and AI-assisted livestock health support so that farmers can act earlier, reduce avoidable losses, and manage animal care with confidence.

Privacy Commitment

Kitsiso is committed to responsible handling of farmer, livestock, and location-related information. Data collected through the platform should be used to support animal health guidance, alerts, records, and service improvement while respecting user privacy and access control.
