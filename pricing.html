Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Kitsiso without internet? expand_more
Yes! Kitsiso is built for the field. You can log all your data offline while at the cattle post, and the app will automatically sync once network coverage is available.
Is my herd's data private? expand_more
Yes. Your livestock data is treated as private and should only be used to support your animal health records, alerts, and app experience.
How does the AI disease prediction work? expand_more
Kitsiso uses logged observations, behaviour patterns, photos, and regional alerts to help identify early warning signs and guide farmers toward quicker action.
What if I have more than 100 cattle? expand_more
The Commercial plan supports unlimited herd sizes and is designed for growing farms, ranches, and livestock businesses.
Can I switch plans later? expand_more
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan as your herd, business, or support needs change.
