Our Mission

Rooted in the Field,
Built for the Future.

United by a mission to protect the backbone of Botswana's economy: our livestock. Kitsiso was born from a deep understanding of local farming challenges. We combine grassroots agricultural knowledge with advanced artificial intelligence to give farmers the tools they need to act earlier, reduce avoidable losses, and safeguard their heritage.

Meet the Team

Why Kitsiso Exists

Kitsiso combines local farming realities, accessible design, and AI-assisted livestock health support so that farmers can act earlier, reduce avoidable losses, and manage animal care with confidence.
